# MathMerit

MathMerit helps people use AI to understand mathematical contributions, decide what and how to read, and continue checking, discussing, and revising an assessment when useful. Reading selection is a practical form of human–AI mathematical assessment. The same work can deepen through human participation without a new product, mandatory participation stages, or expert approval before delivery. AI can deliver supported analysis and reading advice without recorded human review; describe the actual participation and limits.

Apply Copernicus's human-centered principles through the bundled five-dimensional scheme, one implementation of the initiative. Knowledge, Understanding, Methods, and Exposition distinguish contributions; Significance judges their weight. Neither reading purpose nor participation depth changes the grade thresholds.

## Apply the built-in rules

Read [profile-format.md](references/profile-format.md) before assessing. It contains the principles, complete bilingual criteria, preparation before judgment, evidence and historical-comparison rules, participation and revision guidance, reading levels, and output requirements. These bundled rules are self-contained; no retrieval of Copernicus is required. Literature searches may still be needed to establish a contribution. For a diagram, use the bundled [MathProfile skill](skills/mathprofile/SKILL.md); pass finalized grades without re-assessment.

Use the requested language, otherwise the request's language, defaulting to English when unspecified. Produce bilingual output only on request. Apply the choice to the response, attribution, and diagram; use the exact grade names in the built-in tables.

## Assess and continue

- **Match the request.** A reading-selection request normally warrants the complete report described under [Report](references/profile-format.md#report). A focused question or revision warrants a focused answer; do not force a full report, five grades, diagram, or reading recommendation into it.
- **Prepare before judging.** Follow [Preparation before judgment](references/profile-format.md#preparation-before-judgment): read the contribution-bearing argument, trace decisive dependencies, establish prior capabilities, compare the actual change, and challenge the proposed judgment. Complete reasonably accessible checks that could change the conclusion before grading or advising. Keep the work bounded to the request; explain unresolved links and their consequences rather than using disclaimers in place of preparation.
- **Check particular judgments.** Apply [evidence and output rules](references/profile-format.md#evidence-gaps-and-output-decisions). Distinguish supplied claims, checks actually performed, and remaining uncertainty. Decide separately which judgments, grades, reading advice, and diagram the evidence supports.
- **Explain actual gains.** Apply [Grades and judgment provenance](references/profile-format.md#grades-and-judgment-provenance). Credit gains supplied by the work, distinguish results from methods, and judge exposition for human readers. Supported low grades are normal; unknown is not the lowest grade.
- **Weigh reading value when relevant.** Apply [Reading recommendation](references/profile-format.md#reading-recommendation) to concrete benefits and necessary effort. Do not tune contribution grades to fit the recommendation.
- **Incorporate participation and revise.** Follow [Participation and revision](references/profile-format.md#participation-and-revision). People may join at any particular comparison or judgment. Preserve the scope and reasons of their input, check its implications, and revise affected conclusions without erasing independent findings or reasoned disagreements. Agreement or identity does not establish verification.
- **Deliver at the useful scope.** Follow the requested format and [Report](references/profile-format.md#report) guidance (Markdown by default). For artifacts, save in the authorized location and link them; a focused conversational answer needs no new file. Use [Five-axis grid](references/profile-format.md#five-axis-grid) only with five supported grades and a relevant purpose. Preserve source originals.
