# Assessment report and contribution grid

Read the edition of Copernicus needed for the output language through the links in SKILL.md; English assessment requires only the English edition. Its grade definitions and names govern all five dimensions; this reference does not maintain a second scale.

## Unit of assessment

Determine whether the supplied material is a standalone work or part of an interdependent series. Inspect its stated scope, companion-work references, and mathematical dependencies; a series title alone neither establishes nor limits the unit. Include the works needed to support the contribution under assessment, distinguishing them from external antecedents. Do not expand to an author's entire output or to every cited paper.

Record the included works, versions, and each part's role. Assess the combined gains and costs directly under the five criteria; repeated statements or shared proof components do not become multiple gains. Per-paper discussion explains roles and reading order, not separate ratings in place of the series assessment. A request focused on one installment concerns its incremental role within the series, not the value of the whole.

For an ongoing series, assess an explicitly bounded, available stage, without credit for promised continuations. If essential existing parts are unavailable, identify the missing material and apply the submission rule; do not silently substitute an isolated-paper assessment or assume a missing proof.

## Historical comparison

**Establish the context; refine only when it matters.**

- Identify the evaluated unit, its versions, and its broad research period, then compare the relevant knowledge, understanding, methods, and usable materials, including for Exposition. Fix the comparison scope from the relevant problems and literature, not from the desired grade. State the historical context and its limits separately from the assessment date; the present date is not the historical cutoff. Do not routinely reconstruct exact precedence item by item. Clear precedents still require substantive comparison; broad dating is not a reason to ignore them.
- Refine chronology when it could materially change a judgment, especially for dense bursts of related AI-generated works. Inspect versions, content, availability, and relevant formation evidence such as drafts, talks, or communications. Publication dates alone establish neither discovery order nor dependence: human research may take years. Distinguish public overlap, independent formation, and actual gains; neither later publication nor independence alone settles contribution. Identify the gain in conclusions, grounds, or other dimensions, leaving unsupported precedence claims open without requiring a complete private research history. Retain earlier gains in cumulative assessment; do not backdate later additions or replace preprint dates with journal dates. Revision-only assessment also compares with the prior version.
- Read the evaluated text and supporting materials, including the passages carrying each proposed gain; authors’ claims alone do not establish it. Extract changes in conclusions, grounds, concepts, relationships, task capabilities, or access, with their conditions and costs. Trace relevant citations and independently search alternative terms and equivalent formulations. Follow applicable local literature rules, including Zotero first where required. Use surveys to locate context and original passages for decisive comparisons.
- Record each decisive source's version, date evidence, theorem or page locator, conditions, and applicability. Explain “prior result → change in this work,” including any needed specialization or equivalence. Titles, abstracts, model memory, and another AI's agreement do not establish the comparison.
- Seek earlier equivalent or stronger results that could change the grade. Resolve plausible candidates or name the remaining comparison and the judgments it affects. Later historical accounts may verify earlier facts; later new results are not prior knowledge. Keep searches bounded to decisive claims.
- Preserve a compact baseline record: research period (finer dates and formation evidence only where material), scope, sources and passages, search queries/channels, coverage, and unresolved candidates. Distinguish checked comparisons from supplied premises. Reuse suitable existing evidence; if new evidence changes a judgment, retain the prior assessment and explain the revision. Failure to find a precedent does not prove priority. Existing knowledge means what was actually obtained and justified at the time, not every logical consequence of the axioms.

For a series, relate each gain to the research context when it became supported. Preserve gains from earlier installments: those installments are part of the assessed unit, not external precedents that cancel its contribution. Account for relevant outside developments during the series; neither its start nor its completion supplies a single blanket baseline. Later arguments may complete the series but must not be credited to an earlier stage. This concerns the development of the contribution itself, not subsequent influence.

An open comparison may still permit a supported provisional or conditional grade. If it prevents grading any dimension, withhold the assessment under the submission rule below. State novelty relative to the sources actually compared and the search actually performed.

## Grades and judgment provenance

Use the ordered grades and full conditions in the current Copernicus, in the report's language. Justify each grade through the relevant prior state, demonstrated gain, and defining criterion; familiarity of the topic or importance of the object alone does not grade the gain. Do not treat the five dimensions as a common scale. Work with no new conclusions can still strengthen grounds or improve understanding, methods, or exposition.

For Methods, match tasks, assumptions, accuracy, and resources; argument and construction count alongside computation and verification. Weigh stronger assumptions, narrower scope, added costs, and learning demands wherever they affect the gain.

For Exposition, justify the intended human readers and purposes from the work and its context, then hold them fixed in comparison. Consider finite attention and working memory: how do emphasis, sequencing, notation, and the placement of detail help readers grasp ideas and follow arguments? Judge omissions and elaborations by their purpose and the readers’ background, not by their amount. Required thought or reconstruction is not itself an expository defect; exhaustive explanation is not itself a benefit.

Support the grade with representative central passages and a comparison showing how their organization or selection helps readers. Distinguish help supplied by the text and explicitly available materials from connections, motivations, or reorganizations you supply yourself. A fluent AI summary is not evidence that the source is readable. Examine obscured priorities, needless terminology, repetition, or distracting detail where present; do not assume them from length or authorship.

For the highest grade, identify the difficult core content and the expressive choices that make its connections or distinctions accessible without distortion. Completeness, independent usability, or isolated polished passages do not establish this. Improvements in checking and use remain contributions, not mandatory endpoints. Do not require elementary exposition, self-containment, actual uptake, or human approval before making a supported judgment.

Separate the contribution grade from checking status. Report text-based provisional judgments, explicit dependencies, and independent checks actually performed. A conditional grade names its key dependency and what fails with it; it assesses the supplied work, not a hoped-for future result. Evaluate the arguments as far as the available evidence permits, state what remains unchecked, and do not lower a contribution grade merely because checking is incomplete. Known defects restrict the claims that depend on them. A complete set of supported provisional or conditional grades can be submitted and drawn normally. An unsupported dimension cannot be filled by assuming a future proof or assigning the lowest grade.

For Significance, explain which mathematical questions or research conditions are affected and why that matters; neither breadth nor the other four grades determines it. Retain the source, scope, and reasons of a supplied human judgment. Map it to a grade only when the criteria support that mapping; do not silently strengthen it. If an ungraded opinion leaves the grade open, preserve the opinion and identify any AI-supplied grade as provisional. No clear human judgment means an AI provisional judgment, not automatic abstention. Report disagreements explicitly rather than averaging them. Community evaluation of a paper and community evaluation of this assessment are distinct.

## Report

Submit only when all five dimensions have supported grades. Otherwise return a brief “Assessment not completed” notice in the selected language identifying the missing grounds and what would resolve them; do not deliver partial ratings, evaluative reading advice, or a diagram.

Begin with the assessment-status label required by SKILL.md. For Chinese output, use **评价状态：未经过共同体评价**; bilingual output includes both labels. Identify the assessment type as **Contribution at the time**, in the report’s language. Identify the assessed unit and included works/versions, Copernicus document date, assessment date, historical context, and baseline evidence. State documented evaluation sources and scope where available.

Give five brief entries in diagram order: Significance, Knowledge, Understanding, Methods, Exposition. Each needs its named grade, reasons, evidence locators, and material limits; shared checking notes need not be repeated. Include the ordered grade names so the diagram's positions are interpretable. Retain material gains or losses that the grades omit.

End with concise reading advice: the mathematical reason for attention and the theorems, examples, proof ideas, or passages worth examining. For a series, give a reading order that respects dependencies and identify where its main gains are established. Assess value for the mathematical community. Follow the output language selected under SKILL.md, using the corresponding edition’s grade names.

## Five-axis grid

Copy `assets/profile-grid.svg` from the skill directory to the output location. The template is English. Set the visible title and accessible title/description to identify the assessed work or bounded series. For Chinese output, replace the axis names, grades, title, and legend with Chinese; for requested bilingual output, add paired text lines and adjust spacing. The template has five spokes and four nested pentagonal rings; replace all grade placeholders before delivery.

The center is `(700, 450)`. From top clockwise:

| Axis ID | Dimension | Direction `(x, y)` |
|---|---|---|
| `significance` | Significance | `(0, -1)` |
| `knowledge` | Knowledge | `(0.951056516, -0.309016994)` |
| `understanding` | Understanding | `(0.587785252, 0.809016994)` |
| `methods` | Methods | `(-0.587785252, 0.809016994)` |
| `exposition` | Exposition | `(-0.951056516, -0.309016994)` |

Inner-to-outer radii are `65`, `130`, `195`, and `260` SVG units. They are drawing positions, not scores. Map each dimension's four ordered grades to those rings. The innermost position represents each dimension’s lowest named grade, as defined in Copernicus. If governing dimensions or grade counts change, update the template before use rather than forcing the new method into the old drawing.

For an assigned grade, set `AXIS-marker` to `center + radius × direction`, make it visible, and replace `AXIS-grade` with the exact current name from the selected edition (both names for bilingual output). Show all five markers.

Give `profile-contour` all five marker coordinates in clockwise order and make it visible, with a clear outline and translucent fill. Do not produce an incomplete profile.

Keep the title, dimension names, chosen grades, grid, markers, fill, and ordinal-scale legend legible. Wrap long labels or enlarge the canvas. Do not add numeric ticks, total scores, or area measurements. Assessment status, human/AI attribution, and checking conditions belong in the report, never in the image.

Check that all five grades are supported, marker positions match their names, and the closed contour follows all five markers. Render and inspect the output for clipping and label overlap when a renderer is available.
